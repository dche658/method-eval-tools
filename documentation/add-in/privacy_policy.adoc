= Privacy Policy
:imagesdir: ./images
:stylesdir: ./styles
:stylesheet: custom.css

The AACB Method Evaluation Tools Excel Add-in has been developed by the Australasian Association for Clinical Biochemistry and Laboratory Medicine.

We take privacy seriously. This add-in does not collect any information about you or your use of the add-in.

The add-in does not:

* collect personal information
* collect usage data or analytics
* store user data
* upload workbook content to a server
* track your activity
* use cookies, advertising, or tracking tools
* retain any data after the add-in is closed

The add-in operates on the workbook content that is open in Excel on your device. Calculations are performed locally in Excel. No user data is held by the add-in or by the developers.

Your data remains in your Excel workbook and is not transmitted anywhere by this add-in.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact the Australasian Association for Clinical Biochemistry and Laboratory Medicine through their standard channels.
